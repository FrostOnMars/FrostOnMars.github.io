# Squires of Linux

Medieval adventures for curious kids aged **8-10**, where real Linux commands
help solve mysteries and save the day. No installs, accounts, or Linux experience
needed. Play in a browser on desktop or mobile.

**[Choose your adventure](https://frostonmars.github.io/)**

## Two quests to explore

- **[The Market Thief](https://frostonmars.github.io/market-thief.html)**:
  Follow clues through a bustling market to recover the King's stolen goblet.
  Practise exploring folders, reading files, and moving treasures with
  `ls`, `cd`, `cat`, `pwd`, `mkdir`, and `mv`. Discover hidden files with `ls -a`
  and earn a knight's sword.
- **[Bird of the Belltower](https://frostonmars.github.io/bird-of-the-belltower.html)**:
  Ring the tournament bell, if you can recover its clapper from a mischievous
  bird! Build on the first quest with text searches (`grep`, `grep -i`),
  executable permissions (`chmod +x`), and running scripts (`./script.sh`).

## Friendly practice, not a real computer terminal

Kids type commands, explore at their own pace, and use `hint` whenever they need
a nudge. Golden commands can be tapped to type them; Tab finishes names and the
up arrow recalls previous commands.

The games teach a guided selection of real Linux command forms in a simulated
terminal. Story actions are pretend, and scripts do not run on your computer.
Progress resets when the page is refreshed or the quest is restarted.
Quest II starts with the sword already in your bag, so no saved game is needed.
