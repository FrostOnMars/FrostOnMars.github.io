Quest II · Squire rank

Ring the bell to announce the King's tournament! First, chase a pie-hatted bird who stole the clapper. Follow the feathers, search the watch logs, and offer a snack before the royal band runs out of kazoo solos.

New skills: grep grep -i chmod +x ./script.sh
